What · The Ramp

Three phases.
One scoreboard.

Days 1–30

Owner
The player-coach
Not HR. Not enablement. The manager owns ramp outcomes the same way they own quota.
Phase 01 · Foundation

Days 1–30.

Protect the fundamentals first — it's the home plate every win rounds back to. Reps build the mental model of how FSG actually wins, and prove they can carry the voice into a room.

The Institution block
Business Lines, Products, value chain, four pillars: Institution, Enterprise, Brand, Execution.
Story Brand, “The Hero vs. Villain”
Deliver an FSG BrandScript pitch live. Pass/repeat.
Watch, learn, execute
Written debrief after each. What worked. What they'd run differently.
Tool stack proficiency
Engage, FSG APPs, Drive, Slack. Log a mock opportunity end-to-end.
Phase 02 · Play the Field

Days 31–60.

The rep steps into the batter's box with coaching in the dugout — this is where they start to Land. Focus on the reps and develop the skills to win. The first proposal goes out with a manager riding shotgun.

First 20 accounts named
Territory cut, ICP filter applied, top 20 prioritized with manager sign-off.
Solo discovery, coached debrief
Manager listens to one recorded call per week. Coaching is specific, not vibes.
Co-piloted proposal
Rep drafts, manager edits in track changes, customer presented together.
Day 45 calibration
Halfway check. Greens, yellows, reds against the ramp scorecard. Adjust now or escalate.
Phase 03 · On Deck

Days 61–90.

The rep is cleared to step to the plate solo and starts to Expand. Manager moves from co-pilot to coach. The rep owns their pipeline, forecast, and outcomes. Day 90 is a formal review with documented criteria.

Owns the forecast
Weekly pipeline review without a script. Stages, dates, dollars, next-step clarity.
First closed deal expected
Not the only goal — but a meaningful proof point. We celebrate it like a launch.
Peer integration
Joins the field rotation. Presents one account at team review. Becomes a team member, not a new hire.
Day 90 verdict
Pass = full territory release. Watch = 30-day remediation. Off-ramp = honest, fast, kind.
Certifications

Four gates.
No soft passes.

Cert 01
FSG Fluency
Written quiz on services, value chain, and naming conventions. 90% to pass.
Cert 02
BrandScript Pitch
Live delivery of an FSG-applied StoryBrand. Voice, clarity, customer-as-hero discipline.
Cert 03
Discovery Call
Recorded mock discovery. Scored on stakeholder mapping, question quality, and next-step clarity.
Cert 04
Engage Hygiene
Five opportunities logged correctly in Engage. Stages, Business Line: Products, owner/assignment/follow-up, amounts, dates, close-won — clean, clear, one tool to win the game.
Manager Cadence

Coaching is a cadence, not a vibe.

Daily
Stand-up Slack post
Three lines. Yesterday won, today's focus, where I'm stuck. Builds the muscle of self-management.
Weekly
1:1 + call review
30 minutes, recurring. One recorded call reviewed. One coaching theme, written down.
Bi-weekly
Pipeline review
Top deals walked, next-step accountability set. Not a status meeting — a coaching forum.
Day 30 / 60 / 90
Formal calibration
Scorecard scored. Written feedback shared. Rep signs. No surprises at day 90.
